RealignEntangledPhotonSourcePolarization
========================================

``device.configuration``

Realign an entangled-photon source with temporary detector protection and state restoration.

:bdg:`foreground` :bdg:`declared timeout 5 min`

:bdg:`Callable from Open libraries`

**Lease:** devices selected by ``deviceId``.

When the EPS is actively in ENTANGLED output mode, temporarily protects downstream detectors with the source's maximum entangled-output attenuation, searches the internal EFPC settings that restore source polarization, and restores the exact prior output state.
