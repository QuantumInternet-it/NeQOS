5. Experimental recipes¶

Use these recipes to choose a capability and the checks around it. The linked cookbook sheets provide the exact current input/network templates and every optional field. All hardware examples require adaptation and verification on your bench; numerical examples do not require a DeviceOS process merely to analyse already acquired data.

Finite detector observations¶

  1. With safe source power, verify the physical detector-to-Time-Tagger mapping.

  2. Run MeasureCountRates for a finite singles window. Its input specifies Time Tagger channel references, not analyzer movement.

  3. For logical coincidences, use MeasureCoincidences. observations declares each observation ID, input references, logical operator and coincidence window. Multiple observations share the physical acquisition window; they do not represent sequential analyzer settings.

  4. Use MeasureCorrelations when you need a delay histogram, rather than just an integrated coincidence count.

  5. For network-resolved channels on a link, use MeasureLinkSingles or MeasureLinkCoincidences instead of copying detector IDs into a handler.

MonitorCountRates is a series of windows, not a single indefinitely growing count. An explicit windowCount produces a finite ordered series; a null count requires a parent responsible for termination. Its latch policy uses annotation rather than retries. See MonitorCountRates.

Align detector timing before narrow-window coincidences¶

AlignChannels reads live input delays, acquires correlations against a reference channel, selects peaks and optionally applies delay corrections. Choose a reference/target set that matches the experiment, then review acquisition, peakSelection and delayCorrection separately.

For coarse/fine acquisition, inspect both measurement durations, bin widths and bin counts. The total histogram span is binWidthPs * binCount; the bin width is resolution, not the entire search range. A broad coarse search locates the delay; a fine search refines it. The distributed example currently uses 10 s coarse and 30 s fine acquisition, but different bins/ranges remain explicitly selectable. Diagnostic peak limits restrict extra peak reporting, not the acquisition span itself.

Before using corrected delays, inspect the selected peaks, inferred offsets, the applied read-backs and generated plots. delayCorrection.apply=false is an analysis-only timing trial. Use ReadChannelDelays and ApplyChannelDelays for explicit read/apply operations. Do not diagnose a wide coincidence background before checking timing and physical input mapping.

Capture and transfer raw time tags¶

CaptureRawTimeTagStream resolves link sink channels and closes the recording before transfer. Supply a stable recordingRef and choose operation=stream for the canonical lifecycle. record is currently a compatibility alias in this composed protocol, not an unbounded recorder.

The source files are created on the Time Tagger’s DeviceOS host; transferred files appear on the NodeEngine host. Inspect both artifact manifests, file-set members, sizes and digests. A small base .ttbin plus numbered members can be a FileWriter sequence: do not assume the largest member alone is the entire recording. TransferDeviceFileToNode is the reusable transfer capability; transfer is not another acquisition.

Measure background without leaving the source parked¶

Choose MeasureLinkBackgroundSingles for clicks or MeasureLinkBackgroundCoincidences for coincidence background. They snapshot the participating source/ATT state, park the carrier, measure, then attempt restoration before publication. Inspect restoration evidence even if acquisition failed. A failed cleanup needs operator attention; a terminal state is not proof that every parameter was restored.

These protocols do not move the analyzer to an implicit background projection. If the background must correspond to a particular projection, configure it explicitly first. Hold the analyzer in an enclosing parent if it must remain exclusive across configuration and background acquisition.

Single-link and two-link polarization acquisition¶

Use InitializePolarizationAnalyzerChannel when wavelength and factory channel configuration must be initialized. Use SetPolarizationAnalyzerTomographyState for one explicit state. Then select the observation shape:

  • MeasureLinkPolarizationSingles measures six logical projections on one link using detector singles. It need not prepare another link in a reference state.

  • MeasureLinkPolarizationCoincidences scans a target while the reference is prepared according to the requested observation. It is not a two-qubit 6-by-6 acquisition by itself.

  • MeasureEntangledPairPolarizationCoincidences acquires the complete logical 6-by-6 coincidence matrix on the two arms of a pair.

Logical projection order is H, V, D, A, R, L. Physical settings depend on the network topology. One polarizer needs six settings; a PBS can read complementary outputs at H, D and R. A pair therefore needs 36 physical configurations for two polarizers, 18 for mixed polarizer/PBS, or 9 for two PBS analyzers. The exported logical matrix remains 6-by-6. These counts describe simultaneous PBS readout, not every optional acquisition policy.

Where pbsReadout is exposed, simultaneous reads both PBS outputs in one window per basis. symmetrized also acquires complementary analyzer settings so a projection is observed through both physical outputs. Preserve the documented aggregation: a single selected branch can be averaged, while the legacy multi-branch policy sums its selected contributions. Do not compare raw counts from the two policies without their normalization/provenance.

The resolver supports distinct analyzers or separately declared channels on a multi-channel analyzer when the driver implements the required task. This does not imply that every optical-configuration task exists on MPC320.

Compensate with classical H/D probes¶

CompensateLinkPolarizationFromSeparableHDProbes compensates one link. CompensateSourcePolarizationFromSeparableHDProbes applies that campaign to the selected source’s emitted links in a single pass. The source supplies HH and DD probes, the protocol acquires singles and background, reads analyzer axes, estimates retardances and installs the table. The source and ATT snapshot is restored at the end; this is not a request to leave the source in entangled traffic regardless of its initial state.

Set probeAttenuation to a safe supported per-link/global request; do not encode above-limit source settings in the network. Both H and D must remain safe. Carrier changes use the protected configuration path; inspect the maximum-attenuation transition and final applied read-backs instead of relying only on requested numbers.

These campaigns have no automatic convergence loop or internal acceptance gate. Installing/persisting a table is not certification of compensation. For the source campaign, inspect the common source report and its per-link child evidence. Both source-campaign variants create JSON/compact text reports and use comparable public plan/observation/estimate/applied/status keys.

Compensate one arm using entangled H/D probes¶

CompensateSourcePolarizationFromEntangledPairProbes uses coincidences to compensate only the chosen target analyzer relative to the reference arm. It acquires its own coincidence background and conditional H/D scans, estimates the target table and installs it only when applyCompensation=true. Its external-background input is not supported; subtractBackground chooses whether the internally acquired background is used for correction.

Choose referenceUseCompensatedTomoStates explicitly. If the reference uses factory states, the default referenceResetCompensatedTomoStates=true can replace its old compensated table with factory-equivalent states after target installation; it does not reset the target’s newly calculated table. This reset is incompatible with choosing compensated reference probes. Persistence uses the campaign’s persist policy.

With application and the default final-state policy enabled, the target and reference end on H in their resulting respective tables. This analyzer H/H state is separate from the source carrier snapshot restoration. Estimate-only runs do not install/reset those tables. The method attributes observed reference-frame mismatch to the target; it is not a diagnostic that separates source-state errors from target-link rotation.

Measure fidelity after compensation as a separate step¶

Use one run plan rather than inventing another wrapper solely to sequence:

  1. One of the source compensation campaigns.

  2. ConfigureQuantumLinkCarrier to explicitly request ENTANGLED and safe attenuation for the next measurement.

  3. AlignChannels for the relevant detector pair.

  4. MeasureEntangledPairFidelity to acquire the matrix, apply its observation-quality checks, reconstruct density and estimate fidelity.

The compensation campaign restores its previous carrier state, so step 2 is deliberate. Fidelity measurement does not implicitly set source traffic or perform timing alignment. Its choice of compensated/factory states must agree with the tables actually installed on each link. Use useCompensatedTomoStatesByLink when the links need distinct choices.

Inspect labFrameFidelity (the current analyzer frame), fullyEntangledFraction (the best local frame inferred using SVD) and frameMisalignmentFidelityLoss separately. Numerical local-unitary optimization is a cross-check, not a hardware correction that was applied. polarizationDirectionAgreement from one-qubit compensation mathematics is not a two-qubit state fidelity and does not measure depolarization.

For already saved matrices, use ValidateEntangledPairPolarizationTomography, ReconstructTwoQubitDensityMatrix, EstimateEntangledPairFidelity and, when appropriate, DeriveEntangledPairFidelitySummary instead of reacquiring hardware data. Plot/report artifacts are declared on the relevant sheets.

Calibration and alignment utilities¶

InstallPolarizationAnalyzerCompensation writes the requested table to the device and can persist it. LoadPolarizationAnalyzerCompensation loads on that host: a null channel selection omits the filter and requests all persisted channels; an array selects channels. Inspect returned presence/channel evidence, not just an OK command reply. ResetPolarizationAnalyzerCompensationToFactory copies factory-equivalent states into the compensated table; it is not merely a request to move the analyzer to factory H.

AlignSNSPDInputPolarization and AlignMultipleSNSPDInputPolarizations optimize detector coupling from singles feedback. They are not tomography-based link compensation. Their coarse/refinement passes keep iteration evidence and plots; inspect the saved best angles and pass-specific folders rather than just the final value.

RealignEntangledPhotonSourcePolarization is source-internal alignment, not a substitute for link compensation. The current contract requires an already ENTANGLED source and rejects HH/DD; do not assume it performs that traffic transition itself. Consult its source/ATT protection and restoration contract and hardware-specific limits before operating; do not infer its safety sequence from the command name.
