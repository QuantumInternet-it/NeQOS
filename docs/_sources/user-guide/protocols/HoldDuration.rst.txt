HoldDuration
============

``execution.control``

Wait for a declared interval and report the completed hold.

:bdg:`foreground` :bdg:`declared timeout 24 h 1 min`

:bdg:`Callable from Open libraries`

**Lease:** devices selected by ``resourceDeviceIds``.

Wait for a declared duration without issuing device commands, then report the completed hold interval.
