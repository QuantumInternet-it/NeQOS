CompensateSourcePolarizationFromSeparableHDProbes
=================================================

Compensates each of the two selected emitted links of one entangled-photon source once from factory tomography states and publishes their installed absolute compensation tables without post-installation verification, acceptance thresholds or automatic repetition; COMPLETED denotes operational completion, not certified polarization quality. An explicit latchGuard policy is applied to every acquired window; required includes the network-resolved detector-health devices in the declared resource union.

Catalog location: ``link.polarizationCompensation``. This page documents the current exported Reserved contract, not a hardware-test certificate.
