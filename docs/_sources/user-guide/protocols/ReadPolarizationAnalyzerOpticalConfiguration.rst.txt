ReadPolarizationAnalyzerOpticalConfiguration
============================================

``device.configuration``

Read a PA1000 or PA2000 analyzer optical configuration and waveplate axes.

:bdg:`foreground` :bdg:`timeout 30 s`

**Lease:** devices selected by ``deviceId``.

Reads one PA1000 or PA2000 polarization-analyzer channel's cached DeviceOS optical configuration and publishes its validated canonical three-waveplate axis matrix. MPC320 is intentionally unsupported until its driver exposes the same optical-configuration task.

2 What you get
--------------

.. list-table::
   :header-rows: 1
   :width: 100%
   :class: api-table

   * - Export key
     - Content
   * - device.configuration.\ **observation**\ .polarizationAnalyzerOpticalConfiguration
     - Observed DeviceOS optical-configuration snapshot for the selected analyzer channel.
   * - device.configuration.\ **calibration**\ .polarizationAnalyzerWaveplateAxes
     - Validated canonical 3x3 waveplate-axis matrix derived from the selected channel's calibrated optical configuration.

Every declared key is present after successful completion; its value may be null or empty. Read the keys under ``outputs`` in ``result.json``; see :doc:`/user-guide/results`.
