MeasureEntangledPairFidelity
============================

Measures Phi+ lab-frame fidelity, the fully entangled fraction, and their fidelity gap from guarded entangled-pair tomography of the current optical state without configuring or compensating the source.

Catalog location: ``entanglement.fidelity``. This page documents the current exported Reserved contract, not a hardware-test certificate.

Outputs and evidence
--------------------

.. list-table::
   :header-rows: 1
   :width: 100%
   :class: api-table

   * - Export key
     - Role / type
     - Meaning
   * - ``entanglement.fidelity.estimate.entangledPairFidelity``
     - estimate / object
     - Report from EstimateEntangledPairFidelity: labFrameFidelity is Phi+ fidelity in the current laboratory frame; fullyEntangledFraction is the SVD optimum over local unitary frames; frameMisalignmentFidelityLoss is their dimensionless difference, ideally recoverable by local unitary compensation. Independent numerical optimization appears only under crossCheck, with no delta field. These metrics do not imply any physical correction or identify whether a deficit originates in source, channel, or measurement. Null when quality assessment is rejected or malformed and no fidelity is computed.
   * - ``entanglement.fidelity.status.tomographyAcceptance``
     - status / object
     - Parent acceptance decision, rejection reasons, and references to the validator's runtime, result, and quality report. Published before failure when tomography is rejected; no fidelity estimate is calculated in that case.
   * - ``entanglement.fidelity.artifact.densityMatrixPlots``
     - artifact / object
     - PNG/SVG paths for labFrame and the theoretical svdFrame, produced once by the fidelity-estimation child and re-exported unchanged; empty when quality rejection prevents estimation.
