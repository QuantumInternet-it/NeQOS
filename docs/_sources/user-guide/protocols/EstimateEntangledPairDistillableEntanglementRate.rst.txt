EstimateEntangledPairDistillableEntanglementRate
================================================

Estimates detected pair throughput and a best-direction one-way hashing lower bound in ebit per pair and ebit per second from an existing two-qubit density matrix and background-corrected tomography, without acquisition or physical distillation.

Catalog location: ``entanglement.distillation``. This page documents the current exported Reserved contract, not a hardware-test certificate.

entangledPairPolarizationTomography
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

The same source observation used for reconstruction: measurementDurationSec per projection and correctedCountsByProjection. Sum complete HV, DA and RL outcomes, divide each sum by the per-projection duration (not the whole scan duration) and average the three rates; works for polarizer and PBS readouts.
