ReinitializeTimeTagger
======================

``device.initialization``

Recreate the Time Tagger engine and restore its channel configuration.

:bdg:`foreground` :bdg:`timeout 3 min`

**Lease:** devices selected by ``timeTaggerDeviceId``.

Release and recreate a Time Tagger measurement engine, then restore its physical-channel configuration.
