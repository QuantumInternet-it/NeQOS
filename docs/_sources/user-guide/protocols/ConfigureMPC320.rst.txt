ConfigureMPC320
===============

``device.configuration``

Configure an MPC320 controller velocity, home position and jog sizes.

:bdg:`foreground` :bdg:`timeout 1 min`

**Lease:** devices selected by ``mpcDeviceId``.

Applies optional velocity, home-position, and jog-size settings to one MPC320 polarization controller and returns the applied configuration.

2 What you get
--------------

.. list-table::
   :header-rows: 1
   :width: 100%
   :class: api-table

   * - Export key
     - Content
   * - device.configuration.\ **applied**\ .mpc320
     - Device identifier and applied MPC320 velocity, home-position, and jog-size settings.

Every declared key is present after successful completion; its value may be null or empty. Read the keys under ``outputs`` in ``result.json``; see :doc:`/user-guide/results`.
