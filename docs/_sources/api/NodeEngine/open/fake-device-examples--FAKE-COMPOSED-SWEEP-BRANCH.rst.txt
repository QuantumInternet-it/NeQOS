FAKE-COMPOSED-SWEEP-BRANCH
==========================

Library: ``fake-device-examples``

Submits sweepCount leaf runs from one stage, waits for all of them and aggregates their declared outputs.

:bdg:`foreground` :bdg:`declared timeout 30 s`

**Lease:** none of its own; child protocols lease the devices they use.

1 Required inputs
-----------------

Catalog defaults satisfy the input schema; no input overrides are required.

:doc:`Input defaults and launching </user-guide/running>`.

2 What you get
--------------

.. list-table::
   :header-rows: 1
   :width: 100%
   :class: api-table

   * - Export key
     - Content
   * - ``test.sweep``
     - Aggregated deterministic leaf results.

Every declared key is present after successful completion; its value may be null or empty. Read the keys under ``outputs`` in ``result.json``; see :doc:`/user-guide/results`.
