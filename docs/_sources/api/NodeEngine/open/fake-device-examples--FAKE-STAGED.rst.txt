FAKE-STAGED
===========

Library: ``fake-device-examples``

Multi-stage FakeDriver foreground protocol mixing short and pausable long tasks.

:bdg:`foreground` :bdg:`timeout 2 min`

**Lease:** ``FakeDeviceId``.

1 Required inputs
-----------------

Catalog defaults satisfy the input schema; no input overrides are required.

:doc:`Input defaults and launching </user-guide/running>`.

2 What you get
--------------

.. list-table::
   :header-rows: 1
   :width: 100%
   :class: api-table

   * - Export key
     - Content
   * - ``summary.status``
     - Terminal status returned by the final foreground check.
   * - ``summary.deviceId``
     - FakeDriver device used by every stage.
   * - ``summary.gain``
     - Gain confirmed by the preparation task.
   * - ``summary.firstLongDurationSec``
     - Duration confirmed by the first pausable task in seconds.
   * - ``summary.secondLongDurationSec``
     - Duration confirmed by the second pausable task in seconds.

Declared keys are present after successful completion; values may be null or empty. See :doc:`/user-guide/results`.
