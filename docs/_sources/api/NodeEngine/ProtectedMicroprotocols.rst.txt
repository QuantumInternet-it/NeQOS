Protected Microprotocols
========================

Protected templates listed here are explicitly exported and may be invoked by an open meta-protocol.
Templates not marked ``exported: true`` remain internal to NodeEngine.

Source: ``NodeEnginePack/ProtectedProtocolCatalog.py``

MPC-OptimizeCounts-OneDetector
^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^

Automatically align an MPC320 polarization controller by scanning paddles 1..3 and optimizing one TimeTagger detector countrate.

Invocation: ``SubmitMicroProtocolStage`` with this ``templateId`` is allowed from an open pack.
