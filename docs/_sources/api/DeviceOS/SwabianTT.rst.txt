SwabianTT Driver
================

.. admonition:: Driver overview
   :class: driver-overview

   :Driver class: ``SwabianTTDriver``
   :Source: ``DeviceOSPack/SwabianTTDriver.py``
   :Official product page: `Swabian Time Tagger <https://www.swabianinstruments.com/time-tagger/>`_

HardwareDriver for a single Swabian Time Tagger.

The driver manages physical channels discovered from the device, logical
channels created through Swabian virtual-channel primitives, and synchronized
COUNTRATE or CORRELATION measurements. Configured physical channels also
expose runtime parameters named ``channel.<id>.triggerLevelVolt`` and
``channel.<id>.inputDelayPs``.

Measurement definitions remain registered until ResetMeasurementEngine.
ActivateMeasurements selects the subset used by the next StartMeasurement
window. RecordTimeTagStream writes raw selected-channel events as .ttbin.
