Photek Driver
=============

.. admonition:: Driver overview
   :class: driver-overview

   :Driver class: ``PhotekDriver``
   :Source: ``DeviceOSPack/PhotekDriver.py``
   :Official product page: `Photec (Photon Technology) Driver <https://www.cnphotec.com/en/product/snspd/snspd-system#product>`_

Control a 16-channel Photek SNSPD bias controller.

The driver configures detector bias currents, integration time, and trigger
levels. It also exposes count-based latch health. Photon measurements are
intentionally owned by the TimeTagger driver, rather than by this driver.
